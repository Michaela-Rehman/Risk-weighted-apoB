## Overview

The **Risk-weighted ApoB (RW-ApoB) Calculator** estimates your lipid-associated coronary heart disease risk using:

- Triglycerides  
- Lipoprotein(a)  
- Apolipoprotein B (ApoB)  

To obtain RW-ApoB: a novel summary metric which outperforms LDL-C, non HDL-C and ApoB in predicting coronary heart disease

It provides:

- RW-ApoB result
- Approximate LDL-C equivalent  
- Particle and risk contribution charts  
- Percentile comparison to a reference population  

All calculations run directly in your browser — **no installation required**.

---

## How to Use

1. Click the **[Try Live Calculator](https://www.rwapob.com/)** badge above.  
2. Enter your lipid measurements.  
3. View your results instantly: RW-ApoB, LDL-C equivalent, charts, and percentiles.  
4. Optionally, export your results to PDF or share via your device.
